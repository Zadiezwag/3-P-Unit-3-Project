Recipe #1: Spam Musubi

  • Time: 20 min
  • Serves: Humans
Ingredients Quantity
Low sodium Spam 1 can
Soy sauce 2 tbsp
Brown sugar 2 tbsp
Mirin 2 tbsp
Sushi rice 2 cups

Step 1: Cut the Spam into small cubes.

Step 2: Cook in a pan on medium heat until all sides are browned.

Step 3: Add soy sauce, brown sugar, and mirin. Stirr for 1-2 minutes until you
coat all of the spam in the sauce.

Step 4: Cook the rice in a rice cooker and add the spam ontop of the cooked rice.
Top with kewpie mayo, egg, and ganish with furikake.

Extra tip: Fell free to top with an egg or eat with seaweed paper.


Recipe #2:Rice Paper Noodles

  • Time: 10 min
  • Serves: 1 Human
Ingredients Quantity
Rice paper sheets 8
Soy sause 2 tablespoons
Rice vinegar 1 tablespoon
Toasted sesame oil 1 tablespoon
Garlic clove minced 1 clove
Red chili flakes 1/8 teaspoon
Maple syrup (optional) 1 teaspoon
Toasted sesame seeds (optional) 1 teaspoon
Green onion thinly sliced (optional) 1

Step 1: Fill a large bowl with warm water. Submerge one rice paper sheet at a time
for 10-15 seconds until soft (but not too soft to handle)

Step 2: Move the rice paper to a cutting board and stack them one ontop of the other.
Slice them into strips to create a noodle shape. (don't cut to thin)

Step 3: Dunk the sliced noodles into a bowl of cold water to seperate them.

Step 4: Drain to water and pat them dry with a paper towel.

Step 5: In a clean bowl mix together the soy sause, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic,
and red pepper flakes.

Step 6: Place the rice paper noodles into the bowl and toss gently to coat the noodles.

Step 7: Sprinkle sesame seeds and sliced green onion on top for extra flavor and texture.

Step 8: EAT!!!


Recipe #3:Churros

  • Time:20 mins
  • Serves:4 Humans
Ingredients Quantity
Water 1 cup
White sugar 2 1/2 tablespoons
Vegetable oil 2 tablespoons
Salt 1/2 teaspoon
All-purpose flour 1 cup
Oil for frying 2 quarts
Outside coating
White sugar to taste 2 1/2 tablespoons
Ground cinnamon 1 teaspoon

Step 1: Combine water, sugar, vegetable oil, and salt in a small saucepan over medium heat. Bring to a boil then remove from heat.

Step 2: Stir in flour, stirring constantly, until mixture forms a ball.

Step 3: Heat 2 quarrs oil in a deep fryer or deep pot to 375 degrees F. Transfer dough to a sturdy pastry bag fitted with a medium star tip.

Step 4: Pipe dough into whatever shape you want, onto partchment paper.

Step 5: Carefully transport the pipped churros into the hot oil.

Step 6: Cook until golden brown. Transfer to paper towels and pat them dry.

Step 7: Combine sugar and cinnamon in a bowl and toss the churros in to coat them. Serve warm and MUNCH!
